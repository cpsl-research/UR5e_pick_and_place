# UR5e Robot Arm - Hardware Manual

Instructions for powering on and operating the UR5e robotic arm.

---

## Powering On the UR5e

1. **Connect the Power Cable**

   Plug in the robot's power cable. The control box beneath the setup will automatically boot, and the monitor should begin loading the interface.

2. **Release the Joint Locks**

   Once the monitor is active, the robot's joints are typically in a locked state.

3. **Check the Emergency Stop**

   **IMPORTANT:** Ensure the red emergency stop button is not pressed.

   - If the button is pressed down, rotate it while applying gentle pressure to release it.
   - The robot cannot be activated while the emergency stop is engaged.

   **SAFETY NOTE:** Always keep the emergency stop accessible during operation. It is crucial for halting the robot immediately if it performs unintended or hazardous movements.

---

## Activating the Robot Arm

1. On the monitor interface, press the green/red button located at the bottom left corner of the screen to begin the activation sequence.

2. Follow the on-screen prompts to power on the robot arm.

3. Once active, you can use the controls in the top-left corner of the interface for operation.

---

## Free Guide Mode

- Enable Free Guide mode by repeatedly selecting the corresponding button in the control interface.
- This mode allows you to manually guide the UR5e by physically moving its arm.
- **CAUTION:** When using Free Guide mode, do not abruptly release the arm. Due to its inertia, this can cause unintended collisions with other components.
